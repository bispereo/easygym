Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to support@lgappforfun.org. We answer in English and Italian, usually within two working days.

Quick answers

My workouts don't appear on Apple Watch. Open Easy Gym on your iPhone once with the Watch nearby: workouts sync automatically. Make sure the Easy Gym Watch app is installed (Watch app on iPhone → Available apps).

Workouts are not saved to Health. Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Easy Gym and turn on the permissions.

How do I back up my data? With iCloud turned on, workouts and history are copied to your private iCloud automatically.

How do I share a workout? Open it and tap Share: the other person opens the file with Easy Gym.

Can I use pounds or miles? Not yet: version 1.0 uses kilograms and kilometres.
